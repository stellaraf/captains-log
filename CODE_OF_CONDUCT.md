# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Welcome different backgrounds and
experience levels, critique the work rather than the person, and respect privacy.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and publication of private information
are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove abusive content and restrict participation. Report conduct
issues privately to the repository maintainers; do not publish personal details
in a public issue. Maintainers must address reports consistently and protect the
reporter's privacy.
